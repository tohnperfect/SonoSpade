\noindent This supplement collects the whole-frame FID/KID scores (Table~\ref{tab:fidkid}), the
training-term ablation (Table~\ref{tab:abl}), the per-tissue label-swap locality breakdown
(Table~\ref{tab:locality_pertissue}), the per-tissue qualitative result (Fig.~\ref{fig:qual}), the
pseudo-label quality of the frozen $S_{US}$ on real frames (Table~\ref{tab:pseudolabel}), the complete
hyperparameters (Table~\ref{tab:hyper}), and the curvilinear remap (Fig.~\ref{fig:curvi_supp})
referenced in the main paper. All numbers are generated from the same result files as the main tables. For the head-to-head, all three translators are trained on the \emph{same} real pool; an off-pool CUT checkpoint wrongly reverses the result.
